---
name: Account-wide game progress
description: Product decisions for Tap to Earn account saves and the Grand Challenge reward.
---

Tap to Earn's owner chose full account-based save sync across devices and one Grand Challenge claim per account for 1,000,000 coins. The three daily mission milestones can be completed across different days.

**Why:** Progress and reward eligibility must follow the player's account between devices; the milestones should not require one unusually long play session.

**How to apply:** Keep the complete game save account-scoped, enforce the one-time reward on the server, and accumulate the three milestones across days unless the owner changes this decision.
